Excel Formulations & Interview Best Practices

Formulas
1. Dynamic Customer Lookup (XLOOKUP)
=XLOOKUP(B2, Customers!A:A, Customers!E:E, "Unknown City", 0)

Bidirectional lookup returning customer city without hardcoded column index numbers.

2. Conditional Net Revenue (SUMIFS)
=SUMIFS(Orders!M:M, Orders!E:E, ">=2024-01-01", Orders!G:G, "Delivered")

Aggregates net billed total strictly for successfully fulfilled orders.

3. SLA Status Categorization (IFS)
=IFS(Q2 <= P2 + 3, "On-Time", Q2 <= P2 + 15, "Minor Delay", TRUE, "Severe Breach")

Evaluates actual trip time against initial estimated ETA.

Production DAX Measures (30+ Catalog)

VertiPaq Optimized
[Net Delivered GMV] Currency
CALCULATE(SUM(Fact_Orders[total_amount]), Fact_Orders[order_status] = "Delivered")
[SLA Compliance Rate %] Percentage
DIVIDE(CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(Fact_Deliveries), Fact_Deliveries[sla_status] = "On-Time"), COUNTROWS(Fact_Deliveries), 0)
[MoM GMV Growth %] Time Intelligence
VAR PrevGMV = CALCULATE([Net Delivered GMV], DATEADD(Dim_Date[Date], -1, MONTH)) RETURN DIVIDE([Net Delivered GMV] - PrevGMV, PrevGMV, 0)
[Repeat Customer Rate %] Behavioral
VAR RepeatUsers = COUNTROWS(FILTER(VALUES(Fact_Orders[customer_id]), CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(Fact_Orders), Fact_Orders[order_status] = "Delivered") > 1)) RETURN DIVIDE(RepeatUsers, [Active Transacting Customers], 0)

Business Requirements Document (BRD) - Food Delivery Analytics

Objective: Deliver end-to-end data pipeline and visual executive reporting for order fulfillment, SLA bottlenecks, and revenue take-rates.

Strategic OKRs:

  • Reduce Order Cancellation Rate from 8.1% to < 4.5% (recovering ₹1.2M lost GMV).
  • Elevate On-Time Delivery SLA from 78.4% to > 88.0% by mitigating rain and traffic bottlenecks.
  • Optimize Unit Economics: Boost net platform take-rate from 18.2% to 21.5%.

Key Executive Findings & Actionable Levers

  1. Weather Vulnerability: Rainy conditions trigger a +12.4 min delay, dropping SLA to 61.5%. Solution: Dynamic weather buffer (+10m) + ₹25 rider rain surge.
  2. Cancellation Friction: 35.2% cancellations stem from Late Rider Assignment. Solution: Pre-dispatch riders when kitchen prep hits 70%.
  3. Gold Loyalty Tier: Gold members contribute 41.8% of GMV with a 24% higher AOV. Solution: Convert top regulars with discounted annual Gold passes.

Top Business Analyst Interview Questions on this Project

Q: How did you calculate MoM Growth in SQL?

A: Using the LAG() window function over the partitioned monthly GMV CTE to compare current month's revenue against prior month without collapsing rows.

Q: Why Star Schema over Snowflake in Power BI?

A: Star schema reduces joins, maximizing compression in VertiPaq and simplifying DAX filter context traversal.
